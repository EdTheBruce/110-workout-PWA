The 1-10 Method System Kace Calisthenics

Progress does not come from doing more. It comes from doing less, better, and more often. All you need is 10 minutes.

The 3 Rules of Progression

  1. Increase time first: Extend from 10 min to 15, 20, or up to 60 min.
  2. Increase reps second: Move from 3 reps/min to 4, 5, etc. (up to 10 reps).
  3. Increase variation last: Only move from Level 1 to Level 2/3 when time and reps feel easy and controlled.
